HR teams manage work across every stage of the employee life cycle , from attracting candidates to supporting departing employees. The right templates can help you bring more structure, consistency, and speed to that work.

This free mega bundle of human resources templates gives you practical, ready-to-use resources to help bring more structure to your hiring, onboarding, employee feedback , development, and offboarding without starting from scratch.

## What’s included in the free HR templates mega bundle?

The bundle is organized by employee life cycle stage, so you can quickly find the right resource for the task at hand.

### 1. Attraction: Plan and attract the right talent

Before hiring starts, you need clarity on the roles, skills, and candidate profiles your organization needs.

This section includes templates such as the talent acquisition strategy template, candidate persona template, and job profile template.

Together, they help you define hiring priorities, understand your ideal candidates, and connect open roles to business needs.

### 2. Recruitment: Hire with more structure and consistency

A structured recruitment process helps you make better hiring decisions. It also creates a fairer, more consistent candidate experience.

The bundle includes practical recruitment templates such as:

- Job description template

- Job posting template

- Interview evaluation form template

- Shortlisting matrix

- Offer letter template

- Candidate experience survey template

These resources support every step from writing job posts to comparing candidates, collecting interview feedback, and improving the candidate journey.

### 3. Onboarding: Set new hires up for success

A strong onboarding process helps new employees understand their role, connect with the organization, and become productive faster.

The template collection includes the employee onboarding checklist, 30-60-90 day plan template, new employee training checklist, and employee information form.

You can use these templates to align with your HR team and managers on key onboarding steps, role-specific goals, training plans, and employee information from day one.

### 4. Retention: Listen to employees and act on feedback

Retention starts with understanding what employees need to stay engaged, motivated, and supported.

The bundle includes:

- Employee engagement survey template

- Stay interview template

- Employee satisfaction survey

- Pulse survey template

- Employee motivation survey.

### 5. Development: Build skills and support performance

Employee development helps people grow in their roles and prepares your organization for future talent needs.

This section includes :

- Skills gap analysis template

- Succession planning template

- Training needs analysis template

- Performance review template, and;

- Performance improvement plan template.

Each template gives you a practical way to guide growth conversations, plan learning, assess skills, and support performance improvement.

### 6. Offboarding: Manage exits professionally

A clear offboarding process protects the organization and enhances the experience for departing employees.

The bundle includes:

- Employee offboarding checklist

- Exit interview template

- Employee termination checklist, and;

- Termination letter template.

### 7. Cross-lifecycle: Improve HR decision-making

The template collection also includes cross-lifecycle tools such as the decision matrix template and a change management plan template. You can use them to make better decisions, prioritize HR work, and manage change across different stages of the employee life cycle.

## Practical tools for every HR moment

HR teams support employees, managers, and organizations through many important moments. This free bundle brings together ready-to-use human resource templates to help you organize key tasks with less manual work.
